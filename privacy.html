ResultPing Privacy Policy

Last updated November 6, 2026.

ResultPing ("we") is operated by James Spikeston, a sole proprietor in Denton, Texas. This policy covers the ResultPing website and text message alerts sent from +1 (817) 567-8948.

What we collect

  • Your mobile phone number and your opt-in and opt-out history.
  • For connected accounts, the notification details needed to write an alert (for example, a result's name and whether it is flagged).

How we use it

Only to send the alerts you signed up for, to answer HELP requests, and to honor STOP requests. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising. Texts are delivered by our messaging provider, Twilio, which processes your number and message content only to deliver them.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Your choices

Reply STOP to any message to stop all texts. Reply HELP for help. To have your data deleted, email jr@spikeston.com.

Security

Account data is stored on a private, access-controlled server and is not published.
