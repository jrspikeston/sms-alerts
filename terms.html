ResultPing Terms and Conditions

Last updated November 6, 2026.

  1. Program. ResultPing sends automated text alerts when new lab results or doctor messages appear in a connected patient portal account. Texts come from +1 (817) 567-8948.
  2. Signing up. Text START to +1 (817) 567-8948. You will get a confirmation text.
  3. Frequency. Message frequency varies, usually a few messages per month, sent only when something new posts.
  4. Cost. ResultPing is free during the private beta. Message and data rates may apply.
  5. Cancel. Reply STOP at any time. You will get one confirmation text and no further messages. Text START to rejoin.
  6. Help. Reply HELP, or email jr@spikeston.com.
  7. Not medical advice. Alerts are notices that something is available in your portal. They are not a diagnosis. For an emergency, call 911.
  8. Delivery. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  9. Privacy. See the Privacy Policy.
