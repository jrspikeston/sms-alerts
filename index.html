ResultPing

A text message when new lab results or doctor messages land in your patient portal.

Patient portals post results quietly. ResultPing watches a connected portal account and sends a short text when something new appears, flagged routine, follow-up, or urgent, so a high value doesn't sit unread overnight.

How it works

  1. Connect your patient portal account (private beta: accounts are connected by hand).
  2. Opt in to texts (below).
  3. Get a one-line text when a new result or message posts. Full details stay in the portal.
